# GEO citability checklist

Score each page or section from 0 (missing) to 2 (strong). Add evidence, not just a score.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct answer | No answer or buried answer | Answer exists but is indirect | Clear answer near the relevant heading |
| Self-contained passage | Requires surrounding context | Partly understandable alone | Quotable without losing meaning |
| Entity clarity | Ambiguous brand/product/topic | Some context is present | Names, category, audience, and relationships are explicit |
| Evidence | Unsupported claims | General references | Specific sources, dates, authors, data, or first-hand proof |
| Freshness | No date or stale claims | Date exists but scope is unclear | Updated date and time-sensitive scope are clear |
| Structure | Dense or inconsistent | Basic headings/lists | Question-led headings, tables, definitions, and concise blocks |
| Authority | No external corroboration | Limited presence | Trusted third-party evidence and consistent entity signals |

Rewrite high-value sections to lead with the answer, define the subject, state scope and date, support material claims, and link to authoritative detail. Do not equate an AI citation with endorsement or conversion intent.

When measuring platforms, preserve the exact query, platform, date, locale, answer, visible citations, expected URL, and whether the result was a direct citation, mention, recommendation, or omission.
