# Content, authority, and programmatic page systems

## Content authority workflow

1. Identify the query, audience, funnel stage, and expected page type.
2. Inspect the SERP or supplied competitor set for recurring formats, questions, proof, and unmet needs.
3. Evaluate intent fit, expertise, originality, freshness, evidence, authorship, and conversion path.
4. Build a cluster or brief with a distinct primary intent, supporting questions, internal-link targets, and proof requirements.
5. Rewrite for both readers and retrieval: direct answer first, descriptive headings, concise definitions, useful tables, dated claims, and clear sources.

Do not treat keyword density as a quality target. Do not create pages solely because a keyword variant exists.

## Programmatic SEO gates

Before generating pages at scale, define:

- entity and data source, freshness and ownership;
- unique value beyond a template swap;
- URL, canonical, index/noindex, pagination, and sitemap rules;
- internal-link and breadcrumb paths;
- safeguards for empty, duplicate, stale, unsafe, or low-demand records;
- rendered-template QA for metadata, content, schema, links, and performance.

If a page cannot provide distinct value or a safe quality state, keep it out of the index. Re-check indexation and organic performance after rollout.

## Competitive pages

Use evidence to compare positioning, features, tradeoffs, proof, pricing, and user fit. Make the comparison useful and honest; do not invent competitor claims, scrape private data, or publish unsupported disparagement.
