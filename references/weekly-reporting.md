# Generic weekly SEO/GEO reporting

Use only when the team wants a recurring operating cadence. This is a reporting method, not a product-specific report generator or a promise of a particular dashboard format.

## Weekly contract

Define before reporting:

- reporting week and timezone;
- exact site/property and data cutoff;
- prior comparable period;
- metrics and dimensions;
- source exports or connectors;
- page-set or sitemap changes;
- action-item owner and validation date.

## Recommended sections

1. **Executive judgment**: what materially changed and what it means for the business.
2. **Search performance**: clicks, impressions, CTR, position, queries, and landing pages using the correct source dimensions.
3. **Technical and index health**: only verified crawl, rendering, sitemap, canonical, and index evidence.
4. **GEO signals**: AI crawler access, citation tests, entity/authority changes, or content citability findings when measured.
5. **Conversion quality**: qualified actions or engagement signals, clearly separated from search totals.
6. **Actions**: only concrete next steps supported by current evidence.

## Rules

- Compare equivalent completed periods; do not move the baseline when rerunning the same week.
- Keep raw totals, retained cohorts, new URLs, removed URLs, and membership changes separate.
- Do not call sitemap expansion, sampling changes, or dimension differences deindexing.
- Rebuild actions from current evidence. A repeated action needs a current reason.
- Treat small crawler samples as monitoring unless evidence shows a material, sustained issue.
- Record missing data and confidence instead of filling gaps with invented trends.
