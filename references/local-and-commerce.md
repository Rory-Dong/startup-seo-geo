# Local, ecommerce, and product visibility

## Local SEO

- Confirm business name, address, phone, hours, service areas, categories, and landing-page consistency.
- Review Google Business Profile, local citations, reviews, local intent, map-pack competitors, and location-page quality.
- Use LocalBusiness schema only for truthful, visible details and connect it to the canonical location page.
- Track map visibility, calls/leads, direction requests, review quality, and local landing-page performance—not rankings alone.

## Ecommerce and product SEO

- Make category and product pages distinct, crawlable, useful, and internally linked.
- Validate product name, availability, price, currency, variants, images, reviews, and Product/Offer markup against visible content.
- Keep faceted navigation and parameter URLs from creating uncontrolled duplicates; define canonical and indexation rules before scale.
- Measure organic product/category impressions, clicks, revenue or qualified actions, feed/merchant health, and out-of-stock behavior.

For either model, separate technical eligibility from business value. A valid listing or rich result does not guarantee traffic, visibility, or conversion.
