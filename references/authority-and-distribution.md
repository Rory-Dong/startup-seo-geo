# Early authority and distribution

Use this reference after the website and core pages are useful. Authority work should earn evidence and qualified discovery, not manufacture links.

## Credible distribution channels

Choose channels that match the audience and product:

- customer stories, implementation guides, original research, and expert commentary;
- founder or team expertise on relevant communities, events, podcasts, and newsletters;
- legitimate industry directories, partner pages, integrations, marketplaces, and review platforms;
- open-source projects, templates, calculators, datasets, or tools that provide real value;
- documentation and comparison pages that explain tradeoffs honestly;
- local profiles, professional associations, and regional publications when location matters.

For every channel, define audience fit, contribution or proof offered, canonical destination, disclosure requirements, and a measure of qualified discovery.

## Authority asset checklist

- Organization, product, author, and contact information are clear and consistent.
- Claims have sources, dates, methodology, authorship, or first-hand evidence where appropriate.
- Customer proof is permissioned, specific, and not inflated.
- Official profiles and important third-party pages point to the correct canonical entity or product page.
- Reviews and directory listings are accurate, maintained, and compliant with platform rules.
- Content is useful on the publishing platform; it is not duplicated solely to obtain a link.

## Safe link and mention practices

- Prioritize relevance, trust, and qualified audiences over domain metrics alone.
- Do not buy manipulative links, exchange links at scale, automate spam comments, create fake profiles, or publish undisclosed paid claims.
- Do not request exact-match anchor text as a blanket rule; let the context and publisher determine natural wording.
- Track outreach, contribution, publication date, URL, link/mention type, referral visits, qualified actions, and follow-up status.
- Treat AI citations and third-party mentions as observed evidence, not guaranteed outcomes.

## First 90-day distribution cadence

- Days 1–30: establish entity, profiles, proof, founder expertise, and one or two genuinely useful assets.
- Days 31–60: distribute the assets through relevant partners, communities, publications, and customer channels.
- Days 61–90: review qualified referral/search signals, refresh evidence, expand the strongest topic or partnership, and stop channels with no audience fit.
