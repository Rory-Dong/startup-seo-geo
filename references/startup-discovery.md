# Startup discovery and first keyword system

Use this reference before creating a large content plan or page inventory. The goal is to connect product value, customer language, search intent, and the first pages the team can credibly support.

## Discovery inputs

Establish:

- target customer, painful job, trigger, current workaround, and buying context;
- product category, alternatives, differentiators, limitations, and proof available today;
- geography, language, regulatory constraints, and commercial model;
- existing customer questions, sales calls, support requests, internal search terms, and competitor pages;
- the first measurable conversion: signup, demo, contact, trial, purchase, or another qualified action.

Separate what the company knows from what it assumes. Mark unvalidated positioning and claims that need customer or market evidence.

## Keyword discovery workflow

1. Start with the problem, job, category, use case, audience, alternative, and brand—not only the product name.
2. Collect language from customers, sales/support conversations, forums, reviews, competitor page structures, and available search data.
3. Group terms by intent: learn, compare, evaluate, solve, navigate, buy, or local intent.
4. Map each cluster to a page type: homepage, product page, use case, comparison, integration, glossary, guide, documentation, or support page.
5. Score opportunities using customer value, intent strength, credibility/proof, competition, implementation effort, and ability to measure a qualified outcome.
6. Choose a small first batch that the team can make genuinely useful. Do not create pages solely because a keyword variant exists.

## Early page map

For every proposed page, record:

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Primary intent | What question or decision does this page serve? |
| Audience | Who needs it and at what stage? |
| Unique value | What can this company explain, demonstrate, or prove? |
| Page type | Homepage, product, use case, comparison, guide, docs, or other |
| Primary CTA | What qualified next action should happen? |
| Evidence | Customer proof, data, expert input, documentation, or source |
| Internal links | Which pages support and receive authority? |
| Success signal | Ranking, qualified visit, CTA, signup, activation, or revenue |

Prefer a focused page set with distinct value over a broad inventory of thin pages. Revisit the map after real customer and search evidence arrives.
