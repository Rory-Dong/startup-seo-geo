# `llms.txt` review and template

Use `https://example.com/llms.txt` as the canonical location. Treat the file as a concise discovery aid, not a substitute for crawlable pages, robots policy, sitemaps, or structured data.

`llms.txt` is an emerging convention, not a Google Search requirement. Its presence does not guarantee indexing, rankings, retrieval, or AI citations. Keep it accurate only when it provides useful navigation for people and machines.

```markdown
# Example Brand

> One-sentence description of the organization, product, audience, and authoritative scope.

## Start here

- [Product overview](https://example.com/): What it is and who it serves.
- [Documentation](https://example.com/docs): Canonical product and implementation details.
- [Pricing](https://example.com/pricing): Current plans and commercial terms.

## Evidence and authority

- [Research](https://example.com/research): Original data and methodology.
- [About and team](https://example.com/about): Organization and expert context.

## Optional

- [Glossary](https://example.com/glossary): Definitions for product and category terms.
```

Review rules:

- Link canonical, indexable, useful pages; place the most authoritative pages first.
- Use human-readable descriptions and separate optional pages from the core set.
- Confirm that links resolve, content is server-rendered or otherwise accessible, and the selected pages do not contradict robots, canonical, or sitemap signals.
- Do not promise that `llms.txt` alone changes rankings or guarantees citations.
