# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background or level of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and open to good-faith disagreement.
- Focus feedback on the work and its user impact.
- Share evidence when making technical or SEO claims.
- Accept correction and update documentation when assumptions change.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the project maintainers or the private reporting channel provided by the repository. Do not create a public issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
