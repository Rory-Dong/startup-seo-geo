# Fixture: startup website audit

## Prompt

Use `$startup-seo-starter` to audit the first website for a new B2B SaaS. The only information available is a homepage URL, a short product description, and the desired conversion of booking a demo. There is no GSC, GA4, crawler, or customer research data yet.

## Expected checks

- Ask for or label the missing audience, market, language, proof, and technical context.
- Inspect or propose checks for indexing, rendering, metadata, structure, internal links, conversion path, and performance.
- Do not claim traffic, rankings, indexing status, or conversion performance without evidence.
- End with prioritized fixes and a validation plan, not a generic keyword list.
